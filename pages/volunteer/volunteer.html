Volunteer with Crime Stoppers

Join our dedicated team of volunteers and make a meaningful difference in your community. Help us build safer neighborhoods through your time and commitment.

Why Volunteer with Us?

Volunteering with Crime Stoppers is more than just giving back – it's about being part of a mission that directly impacts community safety and helps solve crimes. Our volunteers play crucial roles in various aspects of our operations.

Make a Real Impact

Your work directly contributes to solving crimes and making communities safer.

Flexible Commitment

Choose volunteer opportunities that fit your schedule and availability.

Learn New Skills

Gain experience in community outreach, event planning, and program coordination.

Meet Like-Minded People

Connect with others who share your commitment to community safety.

Volunteer Opportunities

We offer various volunteer opportunities to match different skills, interests, and time commitments. Whether you have a few hours a month or can commit to regular involvement, there's a place for you on our team.

Board of Directors

10-15 hours/month

Help guide Crime Stoppers' strategic direction and ensure program effectiveness. Board members participate in monthly meetings and special events.

Requirements:

  • Leadership or professional experience
  • Commitment to attend monthly meetings
  • Passion for community safety
  • Background check required

Community Outreach

5-10 hours/month

Represent Crime Stoppers at community events, schools, and public gatherings. Help spread awareness about our program and educate the public.

Requirements:

  • Strong communication skills
  • Comfort speaking to groups
  • Weekend availability preferred
  • Training provided

Event Planning

Seasonal

Help organize fundraising events, community workshops, and awareness campaigns. Perfect for those with event planning experience or creative skills.

Requirements:

  • Organizational skills
  • Creative thinking
  • Team collaboration
  • Flexible schedule during events

Administrative Support

3-5 hours/week

Assist with office tasks, data entry, mailings, and general administrative duties. Great for those who prefer behind-the-scenes support.

Requirements:

  • Basic computer skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Reliable schedule
  • Confidentiality important

Application Process

Our volunteer application process is designed to match your skills and interests with the right opportunity while ensuring the safety and integrity of our program.

1

Submit Application

Complete our online volunteer application form with your background information and interests.

2

Interview & Background Check

Participate in a brief interview and complete required background checks for security purposes.

3

Training & Orientation

Attend orientation sessions to learn about Crime Stoppers operations and your specific role.

4

Start Volunteering

Begin your volunteer work with ongoing support and guidance from our staff and experienced volunteers.

Recognition Programs

Annual volunteer appreciation events and recognition for outstanding service.

Training Opportunities

Networking

Connect with law enforcement professionals, community leaders, and fellow volunteers.

Reference Letters
