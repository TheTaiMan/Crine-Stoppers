Types of Events We Host

Crime Stoppers organizes various types of events throughout the year to engage with the community and promote safety awareness. Each event type serves a specific purpose in our mission.

📚

Educational Workshops

Learn practical safety skills, crime prevention techniques, and how to protect yourself and your property.

  • Home security assessments
  • Personal safety training
  • Fraud prevention seminars
  • Cybersecurity awareness
🏘️

Community Outreach

Connect with neighbors, meet local law enforcement, and learn about community safety resources.

  • Safety fairs and exhibitions
  • Neighborhood meetings
  • Community barbecues
  • Information booths
💰

Fundraising Events

Support Crime Stoppers operations while enjoying social activities with fellow community members.

  • Annual gala dinners
  • Silent auctions
  • Golf tournaments
  • Community walks/runs
👥

Training Sessions

Specialized training for volunteers, neighborhood watch groups, and community organizations.

  • Volunteer orientation
  • Neighborhood watch setup
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Leadership development
