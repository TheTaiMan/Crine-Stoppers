Who We Are

Since Winnipeg Crime Stoppers' inception in 1984, over 123,000 tips via telephone, WebTips and text messages have been received from citizens. Winnipeg Crime Stoppers is based on the principal that for every crime committed, someone other than the criminal has important information that will lead to a solution.

If you have information on a crime, call Winnipeg Crime Stoppers at 204-786-TIPS (204-786-8477). For residents living outside of the city of Winnipeg please call 1-800-222-8477. Callers never have to reveal their identity. If your tip is helpful, you are eligible to receive an award up to $2000 cash! All awards are paid out using a system designed to maintain your anonymity.

How It Works

Crime Stoppers works because it uses a unique three-way call system and special computer technology to ensure anonymity. When you call Crime Stoppers, you are not required to identify yourself or testify in court. You will be given a code number instead of using your name. If your information leads to an arrest, you may be eligible for a cash reward.

1. Call or Submit Online
2. Remain Anonymous
3. Get Code Number
4. Eligible for Reward
