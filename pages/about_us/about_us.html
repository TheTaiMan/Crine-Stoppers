About Crime Stoppers Winnipeg

Our Mission

Crime Stoppers is a non-profit organization that works to help solve crimes and make communities safer by encouraging public participation in the fight against crime.

Our History

Crime Stoppers Winnipeg was established in 1982 and has been serving the community for over 40 years, helping to solve thousands of crimes and making our neighborhoods safer.

How We Work

We provide anonymous tip lines and online reporting systems that allow citizens to report crimes and suspicious activities without fear of retaliation.
