Winnipeg Crime Stoppers now includes a secure means of providing TIPS through the internet! As a alternative to using the telephone, persons wanting to provide information on crimes or criminals can now also use this special link by clicking the "SUBMIT A NEW TIP" button above to provide a safe secure way to give information without giving up their anonymity.


Special software allows tipsters to simply click and fill in an electronic form which is then submitted to Crime Stoppers. WebTips are completely anonymous through the use of MYSQL server technology.
