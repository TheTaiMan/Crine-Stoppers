Types of Partnerships

Beyond financial sponsorship, we work with various organizations in different ways to strengthen our community safety efforts. Each partnership type brings unique value to our mission.

🏦

Financial Sponsors

Businesses and organizations that provide financial support to fund our operations, reward payments, and community programs.

Examples: Banks, Insurance Companies, Major Retailers
📺

Media Partners

Television, radio, and print media partners who help us reach the community with crime alerts and safety information.

Examples: CBC Winnipeg, Global News, Winnipeg Sun
🤝

Service Partners

Organizations that provide in-kind services such as legal advice, technology support, or professional services.

Examples: Law Firms, IT Companies, Marketing Agencies
🏘️

Community Partners

Local organizations, community centers, and associations that help us connect with residents and promote safety awareness.

Examples: Community Centers, Neighborhood Associations, Schools
