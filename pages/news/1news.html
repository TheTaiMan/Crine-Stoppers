January 25, 2025 Featured

Crime Stoppers Helps Solve Major Break-in Case

Thanks to an anonymous tip received through Crime Stoppers, Winnipeg Police were able to arrest three suspects in connection with a series of break-ins that occurred in the River Heights area over the past month.

The tipster provided crucial information about suspicious activity they observed, which led investigators to recover stolen property worth over $15,000 and make the arrests.

Case Results:

  • 3 arrests made
  • $15,000+ in stolen property recovered
  • 12 break-ins solved
  • $1,500 reward paid to anonymous tipster
