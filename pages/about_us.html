About Crime Stoppers Winnipeg

Our Mission

Crime Stoppers Winnipeg is a community-based program that works to solve and prevent crimes by encouraging community members to provide anonymous tips to law enforcement. Our mission is to create safer communities by building partnerships between citizens, law enforcement, and the media.

Our History

Crime Stoppers was founded in Albuquerque, New Mexico in 1976 by Detective Greg MacAleese. The concept came about when MacAleese was working on a case involving the murder of a young college student. Despite extensive investigation, no arrests were made and the case went cold.

Winnipeg Crime Stoppers was established in 1982 and has since become an integral part of the community's crime-fighting efforts. Over the years, we have received thousands of tips that have led to arrests, solved cases, and prevented crimes from occurring.

How We Operate

Crime Stoppers operates on the principle of anonymity. When you provide information to Crime Stoppers, you are never asked to identify yourself, testify in court, or take any action that would compromise your safety or privacy.

Anonymous Tips

Your identity is never revealed - you receive a code number instead of giving your name.

Cash Rewards

Eligible tipsters can receive up to $2,000 for information leading to an arrest.

24/7 Availability

Submit tips anytime through phone, online, or mobile app - we're always available.

Community Partnership

We work closely with Winnipeg Police Service and community organizations.

Board of Directors

Crime Stoppers Winnipeg is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors comprised of community leaders, law enforcement representatives, and media partners. Our board ensures that the program operates with integrity and serves the community's best interests.

Volunteer-Driven

Our board members donate their time and expertise to support community safety.

Community Representatives

Board members represent diverse backgrounds and areas of expertise.

Transparent Oversight

Regular meetings ensure accountability and program effectiveness.

Our Impact

Since 1982, Crime Stoppers Winnipeg has made a significant impact on community safety. Our success is measured not just in numbers, but in the peace of mind we help provide to Winnipeg residents.
