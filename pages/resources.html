Crime Prevention

Prevention is the first line of defense against crime. By taking proactive steps and being aware of your surroundings, you can significantly reduce your risk of becoming a victim of crime.

Home Security

  • Install deadbolt locks on all exterior doors
  • Use timer lights when away from home
  • Keep shrubs trimmed below windows
  • Install motion-sensor lighting
  • Consider a security system

Vehicle Security

  • Always lock your vehicle doors
  • Never leave valuables visible
  • Park in well-lit areas
  • Don't leave your car running unattended
  • Install an anti-theft device

Personal Safety

  • Stay alert and aware of surroundings
  • Trust your instincts
  • Avoid displaying expensive items
  • Walk confidently in well-lit areas
  • Let someone know your whereabouts

Safety Tips

These practical safety tips can help protect you and your family in various situations. Remember, being prepared and staying vigilant are key components of personal safety.

At Home

Door Security

Never open your door to strangers. Use a peephole or window to identify visitors before opening.

Phone Safety

Don't give personal information to unknown callers. Hang up on suspicious calls.

Social Media

Avoid posting about vacations or being away from home on social media platforms.

In Public

ATM Safety

Use ATMs in well-lit, busy areas. Cover your PIN and be aware of your surroundings.

Shopping

Keep purses closed and close to your body. Don't carry large amounts of cash.

Transportation

Plan your route ahead of time. Keep emergency numbers in your phone.

Online Safety

Password Security

Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts. Enable two-factor authentication.

Phishing

Be suspicious of unexpected emails asking for personal information.

Social Media Privacy

Review your privacy settings regularly and limit personal information sharing.
